# Yawnly Support

Yawnly: Baby Nap Predictor tells you when your baby's next nap window opens.

**Contact:** [forgedbyai@gmail.com](mailto:forgedbyai@gmail.com). We usually reply within 2 business days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Open Yawnly → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Yawnly. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Settings → Delete all data removes everything. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

**Is Yawnly medical advice?**
No. Yawnly gives general, age-based sleep guidance from your own logs. If you are worried about your baby, contact your pediatrician or emergency services.
